Connecting Ideas Clearly

Practise connecting ideas clearly in sentences and short texts. Start with controlled choices, then complete guided tasks, and finish by expressing your own ideas.

Quick Reference Guide

Addition

and, as well as, also, besides, furthermore, in addition, moreover, similarly

Sequence

first, next, after that, then, firstly, secondly, finally

Cause and Result

because, so, therefore, consequently, as a result, because of, due to, owing to

Contrast

but, although, though, however, in contrast, nevertheless, on the other hand, despite, in spite of

Examples and Reformulation

for example, that is to say, in other words, that is (i.e.)

Grammar & Punctuation Reminder
because + clause (subject + verb)
because of / due to / owing to + noun or -ing
Although + clause (subject + verb)
despite / in spite of + noun or -ing
Use a comma (,) after many sentence-opening linkers:
However, … / As a result, …
